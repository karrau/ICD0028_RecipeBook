# Lava Cake

## Ingredients

- Dark chocolate
- Butter
- Eggs
- Sugar
- Flour

## Instructions

1. Melt the chocolate and butter together.
2. Whisk the eggs and sugar, then mix in the chocolate and flour.
3. Pour into greased ramekins and bake at 220°C for 10–12 minutes.
4. Serve immediately with ice cream.
